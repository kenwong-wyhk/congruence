SAS

$AB = XY$

$AC = XZ$

ASA

$AB = XY$

$\angle ABC = \angle XYZ$

The side is between the two angles.

5.2 | 3 ASA

AAS

angle, angle, side

$\angle BAC = \angle YXZ$

$\angle ABC = \angle XYZ$

$BC = YZ$

The side is not between the two angles.

5.2 | 4 AAS

RHS

right angle, hypotenuse, side

$\angle ACB = \angle XZY = 90^\circ$

$AB = XY$  (the hypotenuses)

$BC = YZ$

The hypotenuse is the side opposite the right angle.

5.2 | Writing a proof

1

Name the two triangles first.

"In $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle XYZ$,"

2

Every line needs a reason.

A fact in the question is given. A side both triangles share is a common side: $BC = CB$.

3

Match the vertex order in the conclusion.

$\triangle ABC \cong \triangle XYZ$ means $A \leftrightarrow X$, $B \leftrightarrow Y$, $C \leftrightarrow Z$.

4

The condition goes on the last line only.

$\therefore \triangle ABC \cong \triangle XYZ$  (SSS)

1. Kelvin is wrong.

5.2 | RHS: solution (b)

1

Five conditions: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, RHS.

Any one of them proves two triangles congruent.

2

Check where the parts are.

SAS: the angle is between the sides. ASA: the side is between the angles. AAS: it is not. RHS: the hypotenuses are equal.

3

SSA and AAA prove nothing.

SSA can give two different triangles. AAA gives the same shape, maybe not the same size.
